Offer review mode

Mockup for the Today/email design pass. Offers from the same role gather behind one door on Today. Tap it to review them side by side, keep the Finds you want, and trash the batch.

The door

A door appears on Today only while a role has offers you haven't reviewed, so Today stays finite. Review mode is for offer roles only: Wine, Food offers, and Offers (retail). Newsletters in those roles stay in their normal sections.

Which door an offer goes behind

Content role, set by routing rules. Promotions mail defaults to Offers (S-t3). Move a wine merchant to Wine once with Move to Section, and its offers come through the Wine door from then on.

Hero image

The first large image in the email Cockpit already holds, picked by fixed rules: skip logos, spacer and tracking images, and anything narrower than about 300px. It loads remotely the same way the Reader does (DECISIONS §25). If there's no candidate, the card falls back to the role color.

Keep

Tapping Keep saves the Find right away, the same act as Save Find in the Reader. So leaving with Not now doesn't lose what you kept. Tap again to un-keep.

Trash all (decided 2026-09-26)

Trash all trashes every email in the batch, kept ones included. A kept Find keeps its name, details and link, which matches Save Find then Trash in the Reader today.

Undo (decided 2026-09-26)

One Undo restores the whole batch: every email goes back to the Inbox, and kept Finds stay kept. Each email still goes through the existing Undo log, and Recent Trash lists them individually.

Where the doors sit

Where the Offers section sits today, after the newsletter sections, following Today's tier order. A door disappears once its role has no offers left in Today. Offers behind a door leave the reading queue and the section rail, because review mode is where you read them.
